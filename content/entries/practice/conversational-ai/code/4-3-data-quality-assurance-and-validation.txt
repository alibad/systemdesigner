Data Quality Pipeline:
├── Stage 1: Raw data ingestion with format validation
├── Stage 2: Content quality scoring (grammar, coherence)
├── Stage 3: Annotation consistency checks (inter-annotator agreement)
├── Stage 4: Statistical analysis for bias detection
└── Stage 5: Train/validation/test splits with temporal considerations
Quality Metrics:
├── Inter-annotator agreement: Kappa > 0.8 for intent labels
├── Coverage: All intent classes have minimum 1000 examples
├── Diversity: Vocabulary overlap < 70% between domains
└── Freshness: 20% of data updated monthly with current trends
