Human-in-the-Loop Evaluation:
├── Expert Annotation: 1000 conversations monthly by domain experts
├── Crowd Evaluation: 10K conversations rated by crowd workers
├── User Studies: Monthly 100-user qualitative interviews
├── Red Team Testing: Adversarial testing for edge cases
└── Accessibility Testing: Screen reader and voice-only interaction
Quality Dimensions:
├── Helpfulness: Does the response solve the user's problem?
├── Relevance: Is the response contextually appropriate?
├── Coherence: Does the conversation flow naturally?
├── Safety: Are responses free from harmful content?
└── Personality: Is the tone consistent with brand guidelines?
